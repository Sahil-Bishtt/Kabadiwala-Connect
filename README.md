<h1 align = 'center'>Kabadiwala-Connect</h1>
<p align = 'center'>Bringing the Informal Collector into a Formal Recycling Chain</p>
<br>
<p align = 'right'>Author - Sahil Bisht</p>
<br> <br>
A logistics marketplace that digitizes the informal local waste picker and maps transaction flows seamlessly to formal e-waste dismantlers creating a transparent, traceable, and inclusive recycling ecosystem.
<br> <br>
An inclusive, multilingual platform bridging informal kabadiwalas and the formal recycling ecosystem through simple, call-based pickup requests. By accepting diverse scrap types and offering a transparent platform along with incentivizing continued participation and enables kabadiwalas to earn additional income upon reaching defined milestones.
